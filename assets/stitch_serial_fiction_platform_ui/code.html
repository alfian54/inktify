Echoes of the Obsidian Throne

By Eleanor Vance

Start Reading

In an empire anchored to petrified colossi, sovereign power is mined from the marrow of sleeping gods. Seraphina Kael, a low-caste seismologist in the shadow of the Black Spires, discovers that the bedrock tremors aren't geological decay—they are a countdown. The Obsidian Throne, long thought a silent conduit for dead deities, has begun whispering back to those daring enough to listen.

Forced into a perilous alliance with a heretic arcanist and a disgraced imperial inquisitor, Seraphina must decipher the liturgical codex before the solstice zenith. As ancient subterranean locks fracture and divine remnants reawaken with voracious appetites, every decree issued from the imperial court tightens the noose around humanity's fragile peace.
