book_2 Echoes of the Obsidian Throne / Chapter 43: In the Shadow of Spire
2,840 words

The gate had not opened in two centuries, not since the third Archon had driven his spear into the obsidian floor of the courtyard and sworn that no mortal foot would tread the lower causeways while the Leyline wept.

Valen stood before it now, fingers buried deep within the wool of his traveling coat. The salt winds coming off the northern reef carried the tang of sulfur and burnt iron. Behind him, the carriage wheels clicked softly as the axle cooled, the tired team of grey draft beasts stamping against the frozen gravel of the high road.

"It will take three men to raise the bar," Mara murmured, stepping beside him. Her amber prism hung loose at her collarbone, throwing erratic fractured sunlight against the corroded iron threshold. "And if the ward still hums beneath the lintel, none of us will walk back through this archway whole."

✦ ✦ ✦

"The ward does not hum," Valen replied. His voice was flat, dried of cadence. "It hasn't hummed since dawn. Look at the masonry along the crown."

Mara tilted her chin. Where the black monolith should have held the radiant sapphire glyphs of the Warden's seal, only a thin line of pale ash remained, drifting lazily downward in the bitter squall like gray snow. The glyphs were gone. Not broken—eaten.

"When the spire forgets its name, the threshold welcomes what the dark remembers." — The Third Liturgy of the Threshold

He reached out, his bare palm hovering an inch above the cold obsidian panel. The air between his skin and the stone didn't resist him; it pulled. A slow, rhythmic tidal suction, like the inhalation of a dormant giant just beginning to stir from centuries of suffocated sleep...
